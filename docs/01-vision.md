# 01 — Vision

> **Authority:** The creator's most recent instruction always wins. Everything in `docs/` is a working plan that can be rewritten at any time. Rules written here are strong recommendations. They are not laws.

## The work

**yoKi** is a first-person, single-take cinematic website. You scroll, and you *are* him. The camera moves through one continuous world built from Yatharth's life:
- the gaze he was born into;
- every extreme he lived at once;
- the force he became;
- the people who marked his body;
- the betrayal that pushed him off a cliff into madness;
- the deliberate cut, and the climb over the dead.

At the summit the camera leaves his eyes for the first time. It rises out of his skull and turns around, and we finally see him: a man standing at ease on a mountain of corpses, moustache tips catching a cold moon, who has cut away what he didn't want and kept the silence he chose.

**Name:** `yoKi`. Nothing else: no subtitle, no "interactive mythobiography".

**Thesis (internal):** *A self made into a world.*

## The promise to the creator

The measure of success is **the creator's own satisfaction**, not traffic, awards or a broad audience. NexusHub was praised and still felt empty. This work must feel like *his*. If a handful of the people closest to him walk through it and understand, it has done its job.

The visual bar is still absolute: it has to look and feel better than **The Monolith Project** and **Artisans d'Idées**. Each frame should feel like a still from an exceptional animated film. The motion should feel like one unbroken take.

## Rating and register

**Mature: 18+.** The work is graphic on purpose. The life it portrays was grimmer than any softened version, and the art says so:
- **Violence and gore.** Street fights with split knuckles and blood on asphalt. A motorcycle crash that drags skin across 40 metres of road. A mountain of real-looking, rotting, entangled dead.
- **Horror.** Psychosis rendered as horror: faces in walls, voices crawling as text, his own shadow strangling him, missing time.
- **Sensuality and sexuality.** The women in his life are rendered as he saw them: bodies, curves, skin, styling, intimacy, a bite. This is shown as cinema, in the visual register of Jibaro, Perfect Blue and Gaspar Noé. It is never a cutaway.
- **Obscenity as art.** The corpse mountain is meant to be arresting and repellent at the same time, in the lineage of Bosch, Doré's *Inferno*, Rodin's *Gates of Hell*, Beksiński, Goya's Black Paintings and Berserk's Eclipse. It should be beautiful in silhouette and horrifying up close.

The entry screen carries one quiet content line: `18+ · violence · gore · sexuality · psychosis · death`. It is information, not a gate.

## What it is

- One continuous POV shot, as in *1917*, *Birdman* and *Enter the Void*, with no hard cuts. Transitions happen through space: through frames, doors, lines, falls and darkness.
- A world that is **painted, not photographed**. 3D rendered as illustration: ink lines, painterly light, paper grain, with saturated colour where life was saturated.
- A **mix of motion types** inside every phase: moving camera, physics (falling glass, dust, blood droplets, bouncing balls, bodies), animated figures, flashes, film-like moments and held compositions. It is a full POV recreation of a life, not five painted slides.
- Real data where it hurts: small **annotations, infographics and diagrams** pinned to the world. Distances, weights, dates, ages, overlapping timelines.
- **Sound on by default**: a living, procedural soundscape. The story still reads with sound off.

## What it is not

- A portfolio, résumé, dashboard, skills grid or product landing page.
- A game: no puzzles, inventories, free-roam or objectives.
- A redemption arc or a sermon. It presents; it doesn't plead.
- A cartoon villain fantasy. He is not crowned, robed or throned. Grandeur comes from scale, posture, the moustache and the moon.

## The emotional line

**Recognition → range → force → bonds and their wounds → betrayal → madness → the cut → the climb → chosen solitude.**

The ending is not "he lost everyone and must live with what's left." It is: **he cut the bad and gained the want. Loneliness isn't the curse. It's the requirement, and he's satisfied with it.** The mountain keeps the cost visible. Viewers can judge him; the work doesn't.

## Parallels (thematic, not copied)

- **Kendrick Lamar, *good kid, m.A.A.d city*:** a cinematic self-portrait where a moral kid and a violent environment produce an antihero, with no verdict.
- **Complete, "Jordan":** judged, cornered, isolating, continuing.
- **Marino & Alexandria, "Lust" (Pride's part):** self-certainty that is magnetic and blinding at once.
- **The Bhagavad Gita:** Arjuna on a field of his own kin, told to act and cut attachment. The corpse field *is* Kurukshetra.
- **Mahadev:** the ascetic at ease on the mountain and in the cremation ground, smeared with ash, under a crescent moon, laughing.

## Principles (recommendations)

1. **Feel first, explain second.** The image lands before any text does.
2. **One line runs through everything.** It is the light path, graphite, guitar string, court line, black thread, shadow, relationship threads, the cut, and finally the horizon.
3. **Every effect has a job.** Spectacle that expresses the life is welcome. Spectacle for its own sake is not.
4. **Contrast is the engine.** Warmth before rupture. Silence before the laugh. Bright before black.
5. **Specific over generic.** The kada, the thread, the scar, the bite, the moustache. The Adidas shoes, the Hunter 350, the ₹350 drawing, the 4'10½" girl on tiptoe.
6. **Perfect execution of the one-shot.** Smooth, unbroken, high frame rate. A single hitch breaks the spell, and fixing hitches comes before adding features.
