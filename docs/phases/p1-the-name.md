# Phase 01 — THE NAME · *born into the gaze*

**Ages:** 0 → 15  
**Job:** He was looked at before he could look back. A child who was framed, flashed and expected, then a teen who takes the frame and writes his own name in it.  
**Palette:**

| Colour | Hex |
|---|---|
| Velvet black | `#08080A` |
| Charcoal stone | `#1B1A1D` |
| Paper cream | `#EDE3CF` |

| Brass | `#D8A24A` |

**Look:** Artisans-style light out of darkness, rendered as painted illustration. Paper grain, ink edges, beams you could touch.

## Entry → 01: through the sigil's eye

The loader's sigil has traced itself and `yoKi` has signed itself (see `06-experience-ui.md`). On click, sound starts with a low breath of air. The sigil scales toward the camera. The camera passes **through the empty eye of the contour**, and its outline unravels into one hair-thin filament of warm light that falls and lands on the floor of a cave. The take has begun.

## 01.1 — The cave

- **What we see:**
  - A narrow passage of wet charcoal stone. The walls are close enough to touch: one wall grazes the lens on the left, painted rock with ink-line cracks.
  - The filament runs along the floor ahead and pulses faintly when the visitor scrolls, like a held breath released.
  - Dust hangs only where light exists. Water drips from above, and each drop catches the filament's light on its way down (physics particles, with tiny splash rings in shallow puddles).
- **Things found** (the POV glances down at each, with a small camera dip):
  - a child's sandal;
  - a crayon worn to a stub;
  - a milk tooth on a stone shelf, lit like a relic.

  Each gets a tiny mono annotation pinned to it: `sandal · size 9 (child)`, `crayon · "flesh"`, `tooth · kept`.
- **Sound:** cave room tone, drips with pitched echoes, distant paper rustle, the visitor's own breath (POV), all quiet.
- **Text (first-person, authored):** after the second found object, low in the frame:
  > *Before I had a name, I had an audience.*
- **Exit:** the filament climbs the right wall and runs into a sharp right angle. It's the corner of a frame.

## 01.2 — The first frame

- **What we see:**
  - A **giant picture frame**, three storeys tall in the dark, black lacquer with a brass inner lip, fingerprints and dust on the glass.
  - Inside: a **photograph of a child smiling**, the "best smile" that ended up in a Colgate feature. Warm sepia, film grain, a studio flash catchlight in the eyes. It is painted and photographic at once (generated image).
- **The reveal:** the filament draws a slow line across the glass, and the photo is revealed *only where the light has passed*, like a scanner bar.
- **The glass:** reflects us, the POV. In the reflection, a silhouette of a grown man stands where the child's face is (no detail, just the outline with the moustache tips).
- **Physics:** dust disturbed by our approach curls around the frame edge.
- **Annotations:**
  - pinned top-left of the frame: `fig. 1 — the smile · featured · Colgate`;
  - small, near the mouth: a measurement leader line, `width of smile: 6.1 cm`. Deadpan.
- **Exit:** a camera flash goes off behind us. A white-out (hidden cut).

## 01.3 — The gallery of the gaze (phase climax)

- **What we see:**
  - A long gallery where frames hang in deep space at different depths, receding: modelling portfolio shots, a stage with a microphone, a spelling bee placard, an olympiad medal on a ribbon, a debate podium, an MUN placard.
  - Each frame lights only when a **flashbulb** fires at it. Flashes pop around us (positional, staggered), each revealing a frame for a beat and then leaving the gallery darker than before.
- **The crowd:**
  - Between frames, the crowd: silhouettes behind frosted glass, phones and old cameras raised.
  - Then the walls themselves: **eyes open in the stone**, dozens and then hundreds, wet, real, blinking out of sync, all on us.
  - This is the horror of being looked at. It's uncanny, but not monstrous.
- **Motion:**
  - The camera walks steadily. Flashes are scroll-triggered at authored points, so each scroll tick can pop one.
  - Glass shards from an old flash bulb fall and tinkle across the floor (physics).
- **Annotations (callouts on the frames):** `modelling · age 9`, `spelling bee · winner`, `olympiad · gold`, `debate · MUN`, `school stage`.
  - A running counter in the margin: `times photographed: 1 … 37 … 214 … 1,000+` (authored hyperbole, tagged `SYMBOLIC`).
- **Sound:** shutters, flash whine, crowd murmur building to applause, then one hand clapping slowly in an empty room.
- **Text:**
  > *Smile. Again. Bigger.*

  This is authored, and appears as if called from off-frame.
- **Exit:** the last frame is empty and lit by one steady beam. The eyes close.

## 01.4 — The house of frosted glass

- **What we see:**
  - The empty frame **grows** into a doorway, and we step through into a corridor of frosted glass panes: a home seen from inside a child's height. The camera drops lower, to child eye level.
  - Behind the glass, two adult silhouettes shout. Their shadows lunge and point. A plate shatters against the pane, and the shards slide down the frosted glass (physics with decal smears).
  - A woman's silhouette slides down a wall and cries. The child's shadow goes to her. This is the eldest child as his parents' support.
- **The wall of expectation:** pencil **height marks**, each with a date and a demand written beside it in adult handwriting:

  | Height | Written beside it |
  |---|---|
  | 4'1" | `top 3` |
  | 4'6" | `doctor?` |
  | 5'0" | `be the elder` |
  | 5'4" | `don't cry` |

  These are authored and tagged `COMPOSITE`. The marks go above the top of the frame.
- **The brother:** a tiny hand appears from the bottom of frame and grips the POV's finger. The brother, 8 years younger. The camera looks down: the small hand, the small fingers. One beat of pure warmth.
- **Annotation:** `brother · 8 yrs younger`.
- **Sound:** muffled shouting through glass, a plate breaking, the brother's breath, a ceiling fan.
- **Exit:** the shouting cuts out. Darkness.

## 01.5 — The name

- **What we see:**
  - A single pane of black glass in the dark. The POV's hand rises into frame. It is a teen's hand now, and the **kada** is on the right wrist.
  - With one finger, the hand **writes `yoKi`** on the glass in light, stroke by stroke, following the scroll. The `K`'s upper arm flicks up.
  - The glass reflects a 14–15-year-old silhouette with the first shadow of a moustache.
- **Annotation:** `the name · adopted at 14–15 · by choice`.
- **Text:**
  > *They gave me a frame. I gave myself a name.*
- **The sigil threshold (01 → 02):** the frame veils to paper cream. The sigil traces, and the **kada ignites**. It dissolves into a ring of light that drops to the floor and becomes the point of a graphite pencil.
- **Exit:** the last stroke of the `K` falls to the ground as a **graphite line**. The black glass loses its surface, and beyond it a **paper world** unfolds upward like a pop-up book in daylight.
