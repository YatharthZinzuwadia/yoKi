# Phase 03 — THE LAW OF FORCE · *the centre of the room*

**Ages:** 17 → 24  
**Job:** Presence becomes force. Rooms bend toward him, people call him, he steps between fists, and then he becomes the fist. Then the force turns inward: pressure, psychosis, the ward. Powerful first, then costly.

## 03.1 — The street

- **What we see:**
  - An Ahmedabad-like night street, stylised rather than literal: shuttered shops, a chai cart still steaming, wires sagging between poles, sodium lamps, stray dogs, smoke from a cart, a puddle.
  - Silhouettes lean on bikes. As we walk, heads turn: not all at once, a ripple.
  - Our **shadow on the wall is enormous**, three storeys, cast by a lamp behind us. Our actual body, in the puddle reflection, is just a man.
- **The shrine:** a small roadside **Hanuman shrine**, painted saffron and freshly sindoored, is glimpsed in a wall niche with an oil lamp flickering. The POV's thread swings forward into frame as he leans to look, and the capsule catches the light.
- **Physics:** cigarette smoke curls, a can rolls down the gutter, moths circle the lamps.
- **Sound:** night traffic far off, dog barks, a pressure cooker whistle from a window, a scooter passing, a temple bell (one strike).
- **Text:**
  > *Rooms changed when I walked in. I noticed. I liked it.*

## 03.2 — Ballers Club

- **What we see:**
  - A hidden cut through a club door curtain. Inside: **Ballers Club**, bodies, haze, strobes, a stage.
  - We are on stage performing (the gig): the crowd's hands up, phones up, sweat flying in strobe freeze-frames.
  - **Strobe = time slices:** each scroll step is a frozen strobe frame of the crowd in a different pose (the stroboscopic look), with blackness between.
  - Our shadow on the back wall towers over the crowd and moves a beat *before* we do.
- **Annotation:** `ballers club · the gig`.
- **Sound:** muffled bass through the door → full sound on stage → crowd roar, all procedural.
- **Exit:** a strobe blackout (hidden cut).

## 03.3 — The one people call (*Sankat Mochan*)

- **What we see:**
  - A stairwell landing at 3 a.m. Phone screens light up *everywhere* in the dark: a wall of buzzing phones, notifications stacking:

    ```
    bro pick up
    where are you
    she left me
    they're outside
    papa hospital mein
    come NOW
    ```
  - Three doors, each a crisis, entered in sequence:
    1. **A friend's breakup:** a man crying on a bathroom floor. We sit beside him, and our hand rests on his back.
    2. **A family fight:** relatives shouting in a doorway. We step into the middle, the two shadows on the wall freeze, and one hand lowers.
    3. **Someone's beef:** two groups facing off in a parking lot under one sodium lamp. We walk into the gap, and the two shadows on the ground overlap, stop, and settle into one line.
- **Annotations:**
  - A call log ticks up in the margin: `calls tonight: 7 · 14 · 23`.
  - Then a small diagram: a radial graph of everyone connected to *him* at the centre, every line pulling.
- **Sound:** layered vibrations, rings, muffled voices (unintelligible), the hum of a parking lot.
- **Text:**
  > *Whenever something broke, they called me. I always came.*

## 03.4 — Force becomes friction (phase climax)

- **What we see:**
  - The same parking lot, another night. A tall man in a white shirt shoves us, and something snaps.
  - **The fight, in POV, graphic:**
    - Our fists come into frame. The **kada** is on the right fist like a knuckle weight.
    - Impact: his head snaps, and **blood sprays in slow motion**. Physics droplets hang in the sodium light, then drop.
    - We take a hit: the frame lurches, red flashes at the edge, a tooth-ache ring in the audio.
    - His head is slammed into a rolling shutter with a metal crash, and blood smears down the corrugations.
    - We're on top of him, fists coming down, his white shirt reddening. Then **our hand stops mid-air** and opens. We stand. Our knuckles are split and dripping.
  - The crowd's phones are up, filming us. The gaze from Phase 01 has returned.
- **Annotations:** clinical labels on the aftermath: `knuckles · split`, `shirt · white → red`, `witnesses · 11 phones`.
- **Sound:** a dull, real punch thud (not a movie punch), breath, the shutter's crash, then a ringing tinnitus that takes over everything.
- **Text:**
  > *The same hands that held people together.*
- **Exit:** the tinnitus becomes a fluorescent hum. A blink (hidden cut).

## 03.5 — The ward (inward force)

- **What we see:**
  - A treatment ward: fluorescent tubes, green-white walls, a single bed. The camera is at eye level, and the room is **slowly getting smaller**: the walls breathe in.
  - This is the teenage psychosis, the treatment and the isolation, shown as **horror**:
    - **Voices become text** that crawls across the walls in many hands and sizes, overwriting itself:

      ```
      they know
      you're not real
      look at the door
      don't eat that
      they're watching you
      ```
    - **Faces push out of the walls** like through latex, mouths moving.
    - **Our shadow detaches** from our feet, stands up, turns to face us and puts its hands on our throat. The vignette closes and our breath chokes.
    - Pill blister packs on the bed. A tray. A nurse's silhouette behind the door window, never entering.
    - The **work grind** bleeds in from the adult years: monitors on the walls, 16-hour clocks spinning, insults in an email font, an empty plate, the commute's headlights sliding across the ceiling. Pressure from every direction.
- **Annotations:** `13–14 km commute`, `12–16 h days · 7 days`, `meals skipped`.
  - A **pressure diagram**: arrows from six directions pointing at a small figure, labelled `family`, `money`, `work`, `friends`, `love`, `the voices`.
- **Release:** the shadow's grip loosens and it sinks back into our feet. A single **seam of light** opens in the wall, and a thread of light is hanging in it.
- **Sound:** fluorescent hum, whispers panned around the head, heartbeat, a long exhale.
- **Text:**
  > *I learned to live with the voices. Some days I still negotiate.*
- **Exit (03 → 04):** we follow the thread through the seam into a long dark corridor of **coloured doors**. The eye-scar sigil threshold is at the start of Phase 04.
