# Phase 02 — THE MANY · *every extreme, the same man*

**Ages:** 8 → 22  
**Job:** Range shown as **one hand, one line**. It moves through paper, wood, string, court, stage and silicon, and every material is joyful. This is the most colourful, tactile and alive phase: daylight after the cave. It is the Monolith-level showpiece.

## 02.0 — The paper world unfolds

- **What we see:**
  - Daylight. The graphite line from Phase 01 runs across a **giant sheet of paper** that is the ground. Ahead, paper landscapes fold *up* like a pop-up book, with crisp creases and cast shadows: hills, a tree, a house, a distant mesa, all paper.
  - The sky is a flat Moebius teal with stylised clouds.
  - The POV's right hand (kada) holds a pencil, and the line on the ground is *being drawn by us* as we walk.
- **Physics:** paper flaps settle with a little bounce as they fold up, and eraser crumbs roll.
- **Sound:** pencil scratch synced to scroll, paper folds, a light wind, a lark.
- **Text:**
  > *I wanted to be everything. So I tried.*

## 02.1 — The drawings

- **What we see:**
  - Three drawings stand on easels across the paper field. Each is big, graphite and photoreal-drawn (generated):
    1. **The family portrait:** mother, father, him and the small brother. Every hair in the graphite.
    2. **A recreation after Angel Ganev:** a hyperreal graphite portrait.
    3. **The first commission:** a portrait in a cheap plastic sleeve, with an anonymous hand reaching in to take it and leaving **₹350** in notes and coins on the easel ledge.
  - The camera glides alongside each, and close-ups show graphite build-up, erasures and smudges from the side of a palm.
- **Annotations:**
  - `family portrait · graphite · A3`
  - `after Angel Ganev · recreation`
  - `first commission · ₹350` (the number counts up 0 → 350 as the coins drop, deadpan).
- **Exit:** a contour from the third drawing is **pulled taut off the paper** by an invisible hand and becomes a guitar string.

## 02.2 — Strings, keys and bellows

- **What we see:**
  - The ground becomes **wood grain**, the top of a guitar so large it is a landscape: the sound hole is a crater, the frets are bridges.
  - Six strings run to the horizon, and the POV hand **plucks as we scroll**. The strings vibrate visibly (shader displacement), and dust jumps off them.
  - To the right, a **harmonium** the size of a building breathes. Its bellows open and close like lungs, and the reed sound is real.
  - Ahead, **Casio keys** are stepping stones. Each key we pass presses down and sounds a note.
- **Annotation:** `guitar · harmonium · casio — self-taught, three`.
- **Sound:** Karplus-Strong guitar plucks (procedural, in tune with the score's key), harmonium drone breaths, Casio square-wave notes. This is the first phase with *music*: notes form a short motif that becomes the film's theme.
- **Exit:** a string snaps loose, falls to the floor, and widens into a painted line. Court orange floods in.

## 02.3 — The court

- **What we see:**
  - An indoor basketball court, empty and huge: gloss floor reflecting skylights, painted lines in white and orange.
  - A **ball bounces in** from off-frame: real physics, with a squash on contact and dust kicked from the boards.
  - The POV hand takes it and **dribbles**: scroll drives the dribble rhythm, and the kada flashes on every bounce.
  - The camera **drives to the hoop**. On the climax beat of the scroll, there's a low jump and the ball leaves the hand.
  - His old, worn shoes squeak on the boards. (The gifted Adidas belong to Noor's thread in Phase 04.)
- **Annotations:**
  - `national level · age 15–16`
  - a diagram overlay of a court play drawn in chalk lines that then animates, like a coach's whiteboard.
- **Sound:** the ball's thunk and echo, squeaks, net swish, a single distant whistle.
- **Exit:** the centre circle lifts off the floor and becomes a **spotlight ring** on a stage.

## 02.4 — The stage

- **What we see:**
  - A stage in a black box: one spotlight and a gel of warm magenta.
  - **Dharmesh**, a fluid silhouette, dances, and the POV dances *with* him. The camera sways and pivots on the beat.
  - On one held beat, both silhouettes freeze into a **Nataraja** pose, one leg lifted with an arm sweeping, for 0.6 s. Then the dance continues.
  - The stage edge becomes a podium. Spelling-bee words and debate fragments drift up out of the dark like dust, as letters: `ONOMATOPOEIA`, `MOTION: THIS HOUSE BELIEVES…`
  - Then the dance-class mirror, where he teaches a row of kids, all following his arm.
- **Annotations:** `stage · with Dharmesh`, `dance · taught`, `debate · MUN · spell bee`.
  - Academic extremes appear as two report cards side by side, one with `98` and one with a red `F`, annotated `both true`.
- **Sound:** a percussive dance loop (procedural), the crowd's inhale, a microphone thump.
- **Exit:** the dancer's arm sweeps an arc of light that becomes a **cable**.

## 02.5 — The first machine

- **What we see:**
  - A child's bedroom floor at night. A **beige PC tower lies open**, its fan dusty, ribbon cables everywhere, a CRT humming, floppies scattered.
  - A **big adult hand** (the father) guides the child's hand with a screwdriver. The child's hand has no kada; the father's watch glints.
  - Pixel light from an old game flickers on the wall.
  - The CRT shows a spinning globe that **zooms down from space to Ahmedabad** (the Google Earth memory), stylised and not branded.
- **The internet bill:** taped to the monitor, a phone bill with a ridiculous number circled in red pen.
  - Annotation: `age 12 · thought the internet was free`.
  - The amount is authored and tagged `FICTION`, shown as `₹ ████`.
- **The code:** the cable becomes a **circuit trace** that climbs the wall. Real code crawls along it in light, as one readable line at a time:

  ```
  const me = many.reduce((self, life) => self + life, "");
  ```
- **Uncle:** a terminal annotation: `role model · Jagrut Soni (maternal uncle) · software`.
- **The work:** fragments of React / Next.js / Node / .NET logos as paper stamps, plus a dimmed **NexusHub** window that flickers and closes. Annotation: `nexushub · praised · not enough`.
- **Sound:** CRT whine, fan, floppy seek, dial-up handshake (procedural), keystrokes.
- **Exit:** the code line runs off the screen and **connects every material** in one sweep.

## 02.6 — One man, every material (phase climax)

- **What we see:**
  - The camera rises and turns. All of Phase 02 is laid out like a Monolith vista:
    - the paper hills;
    - the guitar landscape;
    - the court;
    - the stage;
    - the machine.
  - They are joined by **one continuous line** that runs through all of them, glowing graphite → string → paint → light → circuit.
  - The low sun throws **one long shadow of him** across the whole vista, a single shadow over everything.
  - The line thickens and darkens into a **black thread**.
- **Text:**
  > *Every extreme. Same man.*
- **The sigil threshold (02 → 03):** the frame veils to night blue. The sigil traces, the kada glows dim, and the **thread and capsule ignite** in sindoor-orange rim light. The thread drops and becomes the **edge of a shadow on a night wall**.
