# Phase 05 — THE QUIET THRONE · *the man who chooses what remains*

**Age:** now  
**Job:** Madness first: the bottom of the fall is the inside of his head. Then breath. Then the choosing: what to carry, what to cut. Then the climb, over everyone. Then the summit: the camera leaves his eyes, and for the first time we see him, at ease on a mountain of the dead. He cut the bad and gained the want. Loneliness isn't the curse; it's the requirement.

## 05.0 — Impact · the **shattered sigil**

- **Impact:** black. A single **impact**: the frame jolts, there's a crunch in the low end, and the screen itself appears to *crack*.
- **The shattered sigil:** in the black, the sigil assembles wrong. Its segments jitter out of place, the marks light in the wrong order and colours, and it tears in RGB. It never resolves, and it falls apart.

## 05.1 — The pit (madness)

- **What we see:** we're on our back at the bottom of a shaft that is clearly the inside of a mind.
- **Missing time:** the frame **skips** (missing frames, as in the 2019 month). The scroll moves, but some moments simply aren't there. Clock faces with no hands. A calendar where a whole month is torn out.
  - Annotation: `one month · no memory`.
- **The walls are faces:**
  - Every face from the film pushes through stretched skin-like walls: the flash crowd, the man in the white shirt, Kshitij, Noor, Mishti, Laali, K. Their mouths move.
  - **Voices as text** crawl over everything, in every language he thinks in (English, Hindi, Gujarati transliterated):

    ```
    you're nothing
    pagal
    she never loved you
    they're laughing
    look behind you
    ```
- **Body horror:**
  - Our hands in POV are covered in blood that isn't drying.
  - The **eye scar reopens and bleeds** into the left of frame.
  - Our skin splits along the kada line like paper tearing, with graphite underneath.
  - Our **shadow crawls up the wall with too many joints**.
- **The worst of it:** the woman from the clearing appears behind every face-wall. Her hands press against the membrane from the other side. Then *hundreds* of hands.
- **Camera:** chromatic aberration, stutter frames, inverted-colour flashes on strong scroll motion, tilt.
  - Always **controlled**: no seizure-level strobing. Flashes are capped at 3 per second and are softened (see `06-experience-ui.md`).
- **Sound:** whispers in all directions, a reversed heartbeat, high-pitched ringing, the clearing's warm chord played backward, our own breath panicking.
- **Release:**
  - A hand (ours) **covers our own eyes**. Darkness. One breath in. One breath out (the audio is a long exhale).
  - The hand comes away. The voices stop. Silence.

## 05.2 — The landing · breath

- **What we see:**
  - We're lying on rough ground in grey dust, after the fall. The light is low and grey-white. Dust settles in the air very slowly. Nothing moves for a while.
  - The camera holds, then **slowly sits up**: the horizon levels and a hand pushes off the ground.
  - Around us, fallen at random, are fragments from the whole film:
    - a corner of the giant frame (P01);
    - graphite;
    - a guitar string;
    - a strip of court paint;
    - a cracked black kada-shaped ring of light;
    - a phone with a shattered screen (P03);
    - one Adidas shoe;
    - the peacock feather;
    - a **torn family portrait**;
    - a worn **Bhagavad Gita**;
    - a closed **laptop**.
- **Sound:** wind with very low dust friction, our breath slowing, one distant crow.
- **Text:** (after the hold)
  > *I fell. I got up. That's all that happened.*

## 05.3 — What is worth carrying

- **What we see:** the POV moves among the fragments. Light settles on each in turn.
  - **Picked up:**
    - the **Gita** (the pages flip in the wind);
    - the **torn family portrait**: the brother's half, folded into a pocket;
    - the **peacock feather**, tucked into the Gita;
    - the **laptop**, under the arm.
  - **Left behind:**
    - the shoe: looked at, then left;
    - the phone: stepped over;
    - the frame corner: left in the dust.
- **Annotation:** a small ledger in the margin, written like a packing list:

  ```
  carry  — gita · family · craft
  leave  — the shoes · the phone · the frame · the applause
  ```

## 05.4 — The cut

- **What we see:**
  - We stand. **Black threads** run from our body (wrists, chest, the back of the neck) back down the slope into the dark. Dozens of them.
  - Each thread has a small knot, and each knot holds a tiny fragment: a bit of Noor's gold hoop, a chai glass, a bit of white shirt, a fishnet strand, a ponytail band, a microphone, a flashbulb. Pulled tight, they tug us backward.
  - The **Gita's pages turn by themselves** in the wind and stop on 2.47, seen in Devanagari, small, and never translated on screen:
    > कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन ।  
    > मा कर्मफलहेतुर्भूर्मा ते सङ्गोऽस्त्वकर्मणि ॥
  - A cold **Krishna-blue** light (shyam) glances across the page and then the threads. It is the only time this colour appears in the film.
  - **The cut:**
    - Our right hand (kada) takes the first thread and **cuts it with a shard of the headlight glass** from the crash. The glass is the tool; it's the thing that cut us.
    - It's slow and deliberate. The thread parts, and **a bead of blood wells at the cut end**.
    - The released end whips away down the slope into the dark, and **somewhere below, something heavy falls**.
    - Then the next thread. Then the next. Faster. Some threads we **don't** cut: the thin saffron one to the family portrait, the one to the Gita. They stay.
  - The tug stops, and the ground ahead lightens.
- **Sound:** thread tension creaks, a dry crisp cut, each followed by a far-off heavy *thud* down the slope, and the wind rising.
- **Text:**
  > *Nobody left me with nothing. I chose what to keep.*

## 05.5 — The climb through the discarded

- **What we see:**
  - The slope ahead is not rock. As the light rises we see **bodies**: hundreds, then thousands, piled into a mountain, and we are climbing it.
  - **Graphic and real:**
    - Naked and clothed, men and women, young and old.
    - Skin grey-green and bruised purple. Mouths open. Glassy eyes. Dried blood black at the wounds.
    - Bodies tangled and stacked like Rodin's *Gates of Hell*, limbs over limbs.
    - **Flies** lift off in clouds as we pass. **Crows** tear at them and take off.
  - Our **POV hands grab** wrists, ankles and hair to pull us up. The bodies give and shift under our weight (physics), and a head lolls.
- **Faces in the pile** (we pass them, hold for a beat, and climb on):
  - the woman from the clearing, face up;
  - **Noor**, gold hoop still in her ear;
  - **Mishti**, tiny, curled;
  - **Laali**'s fishnets on a pale leg (Laali is the present, so she is in the pile half-buried: alive? dead? the image doesn't decide);
  - **Kshitij**'s afro in the dust, his face slack;
  - the man in the white shirt, red;
  - the flash crowd, still holding cameras.
- **Earlier versions of yoKi:**
  - the **child** from the Colgate frame, smiling, dead;
  - the **teen** with the basketball;
  - the **dancer** frozen mid-Nataraja;
  - the **coder** slumped over a keyboard.
- **Objects among them:** broken frames, guitars with snapped strings, a basketball, the beige PC, the **wreck of the Hunter 350**, plastic chairs, the shattered phone, the shoes.
- **Mahadev, subtly:** **ash falls like snow**. Far down in the valleys, **pyre smoke** rises from the base of the mountain. This is a smashan.
- **Annotations, cold and clinical:**
  - `est. count: 1,000,000+` (the creator's "standing atop a million lives"; tagged `SYMBOLIC`)
  - `relationships: ended both ways`
  - `selves: retired`
  - `potential: some of it is still here`, pointing to dim **gold glints** trapped in the pile: shimmering potential.
- **Sound:** wind over the pile, flies, crows, flesh shifting, our breath steady and *not* desperate. This is a climb, not an escape.
- **Text:**
  > *People I left. People who left me. Every version of me I outgrew.*

## 05.6 — The summit (the reveal)

- **The crest:**
  - The slope flattens. Night has fallen during the climb: indigo sky, a **thin crescent moon**, cold cyan horizon light rimming everything, and far behind, a tiny warm ember on the horizon where the clearing was.
  - We stand on the highest point. The camera stops. **Five seconds of stillness**, scroll-held.
- **The out-of-body move (*Enter the Void*):**
  - The camera **rises out of his head**: a slow float upward and backward, and the POV becomes a third-person view.
  - It turns 180° in a slow arc, and **we see yoKi for the first time**:
    - **Standing**, both feet planted on the bodies, weight settled.
    - Plain **black tee**, dark jeans and boots, all dusted with ash. The jean hems are dark with dried blood that isn't his.
    - **Rajwadi moustache** with the upturned tips catching the moon, the brightest edges on him. **Full beard**.
    - The **scar** under his left eye. The **bite** on his left arm. The **black thread and capsule** on his chest. The **kada** on his right wrist, glinting.
    - The **closed laptop** at his feet, half-dusted with ash. The Gita in his back pocket, the feather sticking out.
    - Hands loose, one in a pocket. His head is turned to the empty valley, not to the sky. Not a victory pose. He is **comfortable** here.
- **The laugh:** a **low, private laugh**. It starts as an exhale through the nose, becomes real laughter, and his shoulders move with it. It's unsettling because it's genuine. It echoes once across the valley, Rudra's register, and stops.
- **The pull-back:**
  - The camera keeps rising slowly. The mountain is enormous: the only populated point in a vast empty valley, with pyre smoke threads, the crescent moon and ash still falling.
  - He is small, but **exactly at the top**.
- **Sound:** wind, the laugh, the echo, then near-silence with a single low sustained tone.
- **Text:** (late in the hold, small, in the negative space of the sky)
  > *Not a curse. A requirement. I'm good here.*

## 05.7 — Finale · the **full sigil**

- **The sigil:** beside the moon, the sigil traces itself one last time in moon-white. The **moustache and beard ignite** last. Then **every mark burns together**: kada, thread, scar, bite, moustache, beard, with the crescent above the head. The wordmark `yoKi` signs itself beneath.
- **Hold.** The nav pill expands (the only "end" UI): the sigil, the phase dots, and a scroll-to-top arrow.
- **No** credits tunnel, CTA, share prompt or thank-you.
