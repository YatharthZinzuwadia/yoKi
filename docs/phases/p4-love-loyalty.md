# Phase 04 — LOVE, LOYALTY & THE COST OF STAYING · *the ones who left*

**Ages:** 18 → now  
**Job:** The emotional centre. Every bond had heat, and every bond had conflict.
- Noor cheats, then leaves for Canada, and the road takes a piece of his face.
- Mishti is 2.5 years of parallel lines that stop aligning.
- The group rots from the middle, with Kshitij at its centre.
- Laali marks his shoulder with her teeth and calls him misunderstood.
- Then the clearing: the most beautiful light in the film, and the woman whose two hands push him off the edge.

## 04.0 — The corridor of doors · **eye-scar sigil threshold**

- **Threshold:** the corridor veils to dawn blue. The sigil traces with the kada and thread ember-lit, and the **eye scar ignites**: first cold blue, then a bead of red runs down from it. It dissolves into the corridor.
- **What we see:**
  - A long dark plum corridor where every door bleeds its own light onto the floor: blue-gold (Noor), peach-pink (Mishti), sick green (the group), crimson (Laali). At the far end is one door with **sunlight** pouring under it.
  - Near the start, one door has been **bricked up**. Its light flickers in missing frames: the image *skips*, and the soundtrack drops out for a few hundred ms. Annotation: `2019 · ███ · one month missing`. This is the first betrayal (K.), setting up the end of this phase.
- **Camera:** lateral glide showing several doors, then a turn into the first.

## 04.1 — NOOR · heat (2022)

- **What we see:**
  - Her room at night, tungsten warm. She's on the bed in an oversized hoodie, legs bare, laughing, pulling us down by the collar.
  - Her body is thick, soft and curved. She is shown as he remembers her: close, generous, the weight of her against us, gold hoops, perfume haze rendered as warm grain. Intimate and sensual, framed with skin, breath, hands and shadows on the wall.
- **Then the phone:**
  - 2 a.m. Her phone lights up on the nightstand. Messages from someone else. The POV reads them (authored).
  - Screaming. The phone is thrown and **shatters against the wall**, its glass spraying in slow motion.
  - Her mascara runs. Our hand is shaking.
- **Annotation:** `2022 · she cheated`.
- **Text:**
  > *I knew. I stayed. Then I let her go properly.*

## 04.2 — NOOR · the send-off (2023)

- **What we see:**
  - Dawn at an airport-like glass wall, huge and blue-gold.
  - She hugs us for a long time. She steps back and walks into the cold light toward a gate marked only by light, getting smaller. She doesn't look back. Or she does, once.
  - At our feet, a box: **Adidas basketball shoes**, her gift. Expensive. Pristine. Cool white light on them.
- **Annotations:** `gift · adidas · basketball`, `→ Canada`.
- **Sound:** a vast glass hall's room tone, a distant aircraft, her breath close, then far away.

## 04.3 — The crash (the road takes the eye)

- **What we see:**
  - The camera turns away from the glass, and we're on the **Royal Enfield Hunter 350** riding home at dawn. Handlebars in POV, the kada on the right grip, the tank gleaming new.
  - Annotation: `hunter 350 · 14 days old`.
  - A one-way road. **An Activa pulls an illegal U-turn** right in front of us. There's 0.4 s of her/his face in the headlight.
  - **Impact, graphic and in POV:**
    - The world rolls, and the bike goes down on top of us. **Our leg is trapped under 200 kg** of motorcycle.
    - **We're dragged**: the asphalt rushes past inches from the lens, sparks fan out, and skin tears (a red smear trailing on the road behind).
    - A **distance counter** ticks in the margin as the slide continues: `0 m … 12 m … 31 m … 47 m`.
    - The headlight shatters, and **glass flies straight at the left eye**. The left side of the frame goes red. The blood veil stays on the left edge of the screen for the rest of the phase.
  - We stop. Silence. The front wheel is still spinning. The sky.
- **Annotations (aftermath, clinical):**
  - `weight on leg: ~200 kg`
  - `dragged: 30–50 m`
  - `face · abrasions · left eye: scar`
  - `shoulder · never healed right`
  - `ribs · leg`
  - `repairs: ₹35,000`
  - `exams missed · passed anyway`
  - `gym: stopped`
- **Sound:** single-cylinder thump; the scooter's horn; impact; the long grinding scrape of metal and body on asphalt, high and horrible; glass; then wheel ticking and birds.
- **Exit:** a white-out from the sun as we lie looking up (hidden cut). We wake in a long warm corridor.

## 04.4 — MISHTI · the long parallel (Jan 2023 → ~mid 2025)

- **What we see:**
  - A very long corridor of **warm peach light**. Two lines of light run side by side along the floor for a long time. That's 2.5 years, and the camera moves slowly.
  - Moments pass on either side like rooms in a train:
    - She's **4'10½"**, on tiptoe to kiss us, her head at our chest, her hand tiny in ours (a scale callout: `4'10½" · on tiptoe`).
    - Two cups.
    - A **floor plan of a future home** drawn on a napkin, which becomes an architectural infographic overlay.
    - Her laughing in an oversized tee.
    - The two of us asleep: intimate, curled, light through blinds.
  - **The conflict:**
    - She asks us to open up. We can't.
    - She says, in the subtitle font: *"Take it to a therapist. Not me."*
    - Doors close quietly in sequence. Arguments in a small kitchen where her shadow is small and ours is huge. Crying under bathroom light.
  - **The end:**
    - We end it. We carry her boxes down a stairwell, one by one.
    - At the bottom, the two lines on the floor **stop running side by side**: one continues, the other turns away. Neither breaks.
- **Infographic:** a clean timeline overlay drawn across the corridor wall.

  ```
  NOOR     ██████████░░░░  2022 — 2023
  MISHTI       ░███████████████████  Jan 2023 — ~2025
  ```

  Annotated: `yes, they overlap.`
- **Sound:** two tones in harmony for a long time; one slowly detunes and stops.
- **Text:**
  > *I wanted a future with her. I just couldn't hand her the key to me.*

## 04.5 — THE GROUP · Kshitij

- **What we see:**
  - A terrace at night with chai glasses, car bonnets and a speaker playing. Six to eight friends laughing.
  - **Kshitij** is the centre: short, slightly fat, a huge afro of curls, grinning, cigarette.
  - Then time-lapse decay in the same space, as the light goes **sick green**:
    - Kshitij thinner, eyes red, powder on a phone screen, a rolled note.
    - Him cheating on his girl: two phones, a lie.
    - **Puke** on a bathroom floor. A nosebleed into the sink.
    - His ambition visibly draining: a crumpled form, an untouched laptop, a bed at 3 p.m.
  - We pull him up by the collar once, twice. The third time **we let go**.
  - **The group fractures:** shouting, a chair kicked over, someone shoves someone, phones out. Some of it is about Laali, some about Kshitij, some about us.
  - The camera circles behind a water tank (hidden cut), and the terrace is **empty**: plastic chairs in a circle, one still rocking, a dead speaker, flies on a chai glass.
- **Annotations:** `since 2020`, `kshitij · not a villain · a disappointment`.
  - A radial graph of the group's connections. Lines snap one by one until only the node labelled `me` remains.
- **Sound:** laughter and music → arguments → flies and wind.
- **Text:**
  > *Some of them left me. Some of them, I left. I'd do it again.*

## 04.6 — LAALI · the bite (now) · **inline bite sigil**

- **What we see:**
  - Crimson and black. A small room lit by a red neon sign through the window.
  - **Laali:** 5'7", medium hair with red ends, an outrageous body in a short skirt, **fishnets** casting net shadows on her thighs, and **long stiletto nails** dragging slowly down our chest.
  - Intimate and sensual, shot like a music video at a slow frame rate: her silhouette against neon, hair falling, nails, skin, laughter in our ear.
  - She bites our **left shoulder**, playfully hard. The camera turns to look: the mark. The **mini-sigil** appears in the corner of frame for 2 s with the **bite igniting** crimson.
  - Later, the same bed. She's turned toward the wall, and our hand is on her hip. She says, warm and certain: *"You're not a bad person. You're just misunderstood."*
  - **The reservations:** her phone face-down; a disappointment in our chest we don't say out loud; we lie awake. A line between us that doesn't break and doesn't pull tight. Unresolved, present tense.
- **Annotations:** `left shoulder · bite`, `now`.
- **Sound:** her laugh, neon buzz, nails on skin (close-mic'd), traffic far below.
- **Exit:** under the door, **sunlight**. We walk toward it.

## 04.7 — THE CLEARING · solace

- **What we see:**
  - The door opens onto the **brightest place in the film**, like Midsommar daylight: an overexposed meadow at the edge of a cliff, tall grass and wildflowers moving in wind, pollen in the air, a white-gold sky.
  - Our eyes take a beat to adjust: a slow **exposure ramp** from blown-out white into colour.
  - In the grass lies a **peacock feather** (Krishna). The POV glances at it and walks past.
  - **Her:** the woman in the clearing stands at the cliff edge with her back to us. Her dress moves in the wind. Her bare shoulders, her hair lifting. She's the most beautiful silhouette in the film.
  - We walk to her. She turns, and her face stays in sun-glare. She holds us. **One long beat of peace**: the sound of wind, her breath, and the score's first warm major chord.
- **Text:** none. Let it breathe.

## 04.8 — THE PUSH (phase climax)

- **What we see:**
  - In the embrace, her hands slide to our chest, **flat**. Her face clears for a fraction of a second and **flickers through Noor, Mishti, Laali and K.**, four faces in four frames.
  - Then **she pushes**. Hard. Deliberately.
  - The camera **tips backward over the edge**. Her silhouette above, looking down, expressionless, getting smaller.
  - The sunlit path line **snaps** at the edge, and its segments fall with us.
  - The fall is a short, violent tumble:
    - sky, rock, sky;
    - the meadow receding to a slit of light;
    - flashes of the 2019 missing month as stuttering frames;
    - voices from the ward whispering *they know*.
  - The **shattered sigil** flickers mid-fall: everything wrong, inverted, tearing. Then darkness rushing.
- **Sound:**
  - The chord cuts mid-note.
  - Wind roars.
  - A single **line-snap** like a cable.
  - Her breath, then nothing.
  - A sub-bass descent.
  - Silence just before impact.
- **Exit:** impact into Phase 05.
