# 03 — Cast

The names are display aliases (the creator already changed the real ones). Where the creator gave no description, one has been authored; he can rewrite any of it. All figures are stylised: painted 3D, silhouettes and partial views. No real photographs of real people are used.

## yoKi (Yatharth)

- **POV for most of the film.** We see his hands, forearms, his shadow, and reflections in glass and puddles.
- **Seen whole only once:** in the summit reveal, when the camera leaves his eyes.
- **Ages across the film:**

  | Phase | Age |
  |---|---|
  | 01 | child → 14–15 |
  | 02 | teen → adult |
  | 03 | 17 → 24 |
  | 04 | 18 → present |
  | 05 | present |

- **Markers:** see `02-sigil-and-identity.md` (kada, thread and capsule, left-eye scar, left-arm bite, Rajwadi moustache, beard).
- **Temperament on screen:** composed, proud, quick to laugh, quick to anger, protective, private. He doesn't explain himself.

## Noor — Relationship A (2022–2023)

- **Look:** 5'5". Thick, curvy, plump and well-endowed: soft heavy curves and wide hips. Long dark hair, kohl-lined eyes, gold hoops. Warm, generous body language. Loves oversized hoodies over fitted clothes, and perfume that lingers.
- **The relationship:**
  - Hot and loud. Intimacy that felt like home, and fights that rattled windows.
  - **She cheated.** The discovery came through a phone screen at 2 a.m., followed by screaming, a phone thrown against a wall, and nights without sleep.
- **The resolution:** acceptance. She is moving to **Canada**. He sends her off anyway, tenderly. She gifted him **expensive Adidas basketball shoes**.
- **The cost:** on the day of the send-off he crashes the 14-day-old **Royal Enfield Hunter 350** (see Phase 04).
- **Visual signature:** cold dawn blue against warm gold. Airport glass. The shoes.

## Mishti — Relationship B (Jan 2023 → ~mid 2025, ~2.5 years)

- **Look:** **4'10½"**, petite "shawty". Thin-to-medium build with real curves. Big expressive eyes, a fringe, oversized tees worn as dresses. She stands on tiptoe to kiss him and her head reaches his chest.
- **The relationship:**
  - The one he pursued for a long time before it began.
  - Deep connection and future plans: a home, a city, names for a dog.
  - He couldn't open up, and she told him to **take it to a therapist, not to her**. That sentence became a wall.
  - Arguments in small rooms. Silences at dinner.
- **The end:** **he ended it**, then helped her move on, carrying her boxes down the stairs himself.
- **Timeline:** overlaps Noor's period, and the work shows that overlap honestly as an infographic, without explaining it.
- **Visual signature:** warm peach and tungsten pink fading to grey. Two parallel lines of light that run together for a long time and then stop aligning.

## Laali — the present relationship

- **Look:**
  - 5'7". Medium-length dark hair with **red ends**, and the hottest body in the film: toned, curved and confident.
  - Modern, revealing style: short skirts, **fishnet stockings**, crop tops, leather.
  - **Long stiletto nails**, always done. Sharp eyeliner, rings.
  - Styling that is never accidental.
- **The relationship:**
  - Affection, intimacy, play, care and responsibility, alongside **reservations and moral disappointment**. Present tense and unresolved.
  - She **bit his left shoulder** playfully; the mark stays.
  - **She told him:** *"You're not a bad person. You're just misunderstood."* (Reconstructed wording of a real moment.)
  - The friend group's collapse was tangled up with events around her.
- **Visual signature:** crimson and black, neon-red bounce light, fishnet shadow patterns on skin, nails catching light.

## Kshitij — the friend (since 2020)

- **Look:** short and slightly fat, with a big **afro of curly hair**. Round face, easy grin, oversized t-shirts, always a cigarette.
- **Arc:**
  - The funniest one in the group, the one who was always around.
  - Then the slide: substance use (smoke, pills, powder on a phone screen), cheating on his partner, ambition draining out of him.
  - Puke on a bathroom floor at 4 a.m. Nosebleeds. Promises.
  - **Not a villain. A disappointment.** yoKi tries to lift him, then stops.
- **Visual signature:** sickly green fluorescent light, smoke layers, flies.

## The Group (since ~2020)

- **Look:** six to eight silhouettes around tea-stall benches, a terrace and car bonnets.
- **Arc:** laughter, phones, plans; then conflicts (some about Laali, some about Kshitij, some about yoKi himself). The circle breaks into shouting and empties.
- **Depiction:** faces are simple painted planes, recognisable by posture.

## The First Betrayal — the 2019 basketball girlfriend ("K.")

- **Look:** athletic, ponytail, team jacket. She is only ever seen in fragments and in missing frames.
- **What happened:** she cheated in 2019. It was followed by a **month-long memory gap**, an episode that is the true root of "descent into madness after betrayal in love."
- **Role in the film:** she is the **flicker inside the clearing woman's face** at the moment of the push.

## Her — the Woman in the Clearing

- **Look:** in silhouette, the most beautiful figure in the film: sun behind her, a dress moving in wind, bare shoulders, hair lifting.
- **Role:**
  - She is solace, the reason to walk into the light.
  - Her face is never fully seen. When she turns, it **flickers through Noor, Mishti, Laali and K.**: love's composite.
  - She embraces him, then **pushes him off the cliff**. It is a deliberate push, with both hands flat on his chest.
  - She watches him fall without expression.
- **What the push means:** symbolically, betrayal in love → the descent into madness.

## Family

- **Father:**
  - An old-era gamer and PC builder: floppies, discs, a screwdriver, a beige tower opened on the floor.
  - His big hand guides the child's hand.
  - In Phase 01 he is also part of the shouting behind frosted glass (parental conflict).
- **Mother:** shouting behind glass, then crying in the kitchen, with the child as her support. Never villainised.
- **Younger brother (8 years younger):** a tiny hand gripping yoKi's finger in Phase 01. He is the family portrait in Phase 02, and the fragment yoKi carries in Phase 05.
- **Maternal uncle — Jagrut Soni:** the software role model, seen as a name annotation on a terminal.

## Others

- **Dharmesh:** the dancer he performed with. A fluid silhouette in Phase 02.
- **The Crowd / The Gaze:** faceless watchers, camera flashes, eyes in walls (Phase 01). Club bodies (Phase 03).
- **The Fight:** one stranger in Phase 03: a tall man in a white shirt that ends red.
- **The Activa rider:** helmetless, making an illegal U-turn on a one-way road. Seen for 0.4 s.
- **The Dead:** everyone, at the summit: strangers, the women, Kshitij, the group, old versions of yoKi (the child in the frame, the teen with the basketball, the dancer), all piled. See Phase 05.
